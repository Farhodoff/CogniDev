Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers about local-first privacy, AI providers, and native macOS execution.

Is my code, voice audio, or task data sent to a cloud server? +
Never. CogniDev is strictly local-first. All your tasks, engineering notes, workspaces, and team groups are stored in an embedded SQLite database (`sqlite:synapse.db`) on your Mac. Voice recognition is performed entirely on your Apple Silicon chip via `whisper-cli`. Only AI queries you explicitly submit are sent directly to the AI provider API of your choice.
Which AI models and providers are supported? +
CogniDev natively supports 6 cloud providers: OpenAI ChatGPT (gpt-4o, gpt-4o-mini), Google Gemini (2.5 Flash), Anthropic Claude (Sonnet 4.5), DeepSeek, Meta Llama, and Muse. It also seamlessly connects to local Ollama (`localhost:11434`) and features an offline deterministic heuristic fallback when no internet connection is available.
How does the local Uzbek speech recognition work? +
CogniDev interfaces directly with `whisper.cpp` through native Rust Tauri commands (`stt_status` and `transcribe_audio`). It loads the localized `ggml-rubaistt.bin` model directly on Apple Silicon to provide instant Uzbek voice transcription without any third-party subscription fees or cloud latency.
Can I backup and transfer my workspace data? +
Yes! With one click or via the `Cmd+K` command palette, you can export your entire database into a portable `cognidev-vault.json` snapshot and restore it on any other Mac at any time.
How do I open CogniDev if macOS Gatekeeper shows a warning? +
Because CogniDev is an open-source project without a paid commercial Apple certificate, macOS Gatekeeper automatically places internet downloads under quarantine. To open it smoothly:

1. Drag `CogniDev.app` to your /Applications folder.
2. Remove quarantine: Open Terminal and run: 
xattr -cr /Applications/CogniDev.app
Alternatively: Right-click (Control + Click) on CogniDev in Applications, select Open, and click Open in the confirmation dialog.
